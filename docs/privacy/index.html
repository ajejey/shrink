Shrink

Privacy policy

Everything happens on your iPhone. Your videos and photos are never uploaded, and Shrink has no servers.

Shrink doesn't collect, keep or share personal information. No accounts, no ads, no tracking.

Photos access is used only to list your videos' sizes and to save the smaller copies you ask for.

If you use Report a problem, your email includes the details shown in it: app version, iPhone model, iOS version and what went wrong. It's used only to answer you.

Purchases are handled by Apple. Shrink never sees your payment details.

Last updated November 2026.
