Shrink for iPhone

Shrink support

Shrink makes videos and photos small enough to send to Discord, Gmail, WhatsApp and other places, and frees up iPhone storage by shrinking your biggest videos. Everything happens on your iPhone.

I used Free up space, but my storage didn't go up

Your originals are in Recently Deleted, so you can still get them back. The space returns when you empty it (Photos > Albums > Recently Deleted > Select > Delete All), or by itself after about 30 days. Apps aren't allowed to empty it for you.

Does Shrink upload my videos?

No. Your videos and photos never leave your iPhone. See the privacy policy.

Get Shrink Pro back on a new iPhone

In Shrink, open Settings and tap Restore purchase. Use the same Apple Account you bought it with.

Cancel the yearly plan

On your iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then Shrink.

Ask for a refund

Apple handles refunds at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Something went wrong

In Shrink, tap Report a problem on the error screen or in Settings, write what happened and tap Send. Your app version, iPhone model and iOS version go with it, never your video. Add your email if you'd like a reply.
